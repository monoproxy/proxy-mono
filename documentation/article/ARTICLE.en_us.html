Proxy Mono is a monospaced typeface in nine weights, from Thin to Black, made by monoproxy, a Sydney studio that builds and teaches.

Every character sits on the same 700-unit cell, and every upright carries the same stem at each weight, with horizontals a little thinner so they read as even. Columns of code, tables and receipts line up and read evenly. Capitals are wide and square-shouldered, the zero is slashed, and there are no ink traps. Proxy Mono is a remix of open-source monospaces, rebuilt by code so that capitals, lowercase and figures share one stem.

It covers GF Latin Core and more: Western, Central and Eastern European languages, Vietnamese, and a wide set of symbols, arrows and currencies.

To contribute, see github.com/monoproxy/proxy-mono.
